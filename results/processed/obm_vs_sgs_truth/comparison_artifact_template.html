SGS vs ALLUVSIM Truths

The existing Gaussian-simulation ground truths next to the new event-based fluvial realizations (ResMill ChannelLayer, CB_JIGSAW preset). A third family repeats the same ALLUVSIM realizations with porosity variation inside the mud facies (FF, FFCH), which ResMill otherwise pins to one value. All families sit on the same 50 × 50 grid with 20 m cells and are affine-scaled to porosity mean 15 % and standard deviation 3 %.
